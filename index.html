🚐 Getting around the Philippines

Understand your options before you arrive.

✈️ Airports and island transfers

Allow generous connections between international and domestic flights. Airports may be far from tourist destinations; check the onward van, ferry or road journey separately.

🚕 City transport

In supported cities, ride-hailing apps can help with upfront estimates. For metered taxis, confirm the meter is used. Traffic can be heavy, especially in Metro Manila.

⛴️ Ferries and boats

Check operators, departure ports, luggage policies and weather advisories. Rough seas can cause delays or cancellations; avoid tight onward connections.

🚌 Buses, vans and tricycles

Routes and fares vary by locality. Ask the operator for the fare and drop-off point before departure, particularly for shared vans and tricycles.
